# What is established

The constructions approach the zero-bulk corner of the solid-void stiffness map. Their shear values are numerical evaluations of explicit multi-scale geometries; the outer bound and zero-mode arguments are mathematical statements with different scopes.

- [Results](results.md): every headline value, baseline comparison and exclusion.
- [Methods](methods.md): tensor conventions, evaluation precision and the support gap.
- [Zero bulk](zero-bulk.md): geometry, compatible dilation and the limiting construction.
- [HS bound](bounds.md): a proof for the isotropic projection of arbitrary effective tensors.
- [Quadratic translations](translations.md): why this mixed-energy bounding class decouples for void.
- [Finite scale](finite-scale.md): what the direct simulations check and what they do not.
- [References](references.md): sources for the mathematical methods and the open problem.
